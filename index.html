Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

RepLift is a strength-training tracker. Your privacy is simple with us: we do not collect, store, or share any of your data.

Data storage

All of your information — workout plans, exercise history, and personal records — is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to any server, and it never leaves your phone.

Accounts

RepLift does not require an account, sign-up, or login of any kind.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect personal information, usage analytics, or diagnostics, and it does not share any data with third parties. It contains no advertising.

Permissions

RepLift does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device features.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of your data.

Children's privacy

RepLift does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the app's Google Play Store listing.
